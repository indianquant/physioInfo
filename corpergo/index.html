Dr. Pinky Dutta (PT)

"We focus on empowering patients through clear diagnosis, ergonomic education, and evidence-based functional rehab."

Dr. Pinky Dutta is the Co-Founder of CorpErgo Physiotherapy & Occupational Health Centre (est. 2013) and Professor & Head of Department of Physiotherapy at Garden City University, Bengaluru. With over 20 years of clinical and academic experience since her BPT graduation in 2002, she is one of East Bengaluru's foremost authorities in occupational ergonomics and musculoskeletal rehabilitation.

  • Professor & HOD — Department of Physiotherapy, Garden City University (2017 – Present)
