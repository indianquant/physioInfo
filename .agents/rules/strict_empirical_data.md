# STRICT RULE: ZERO HYPOTHETICAL INFORMATION

> **CRITICAL DIRECTIVE**: Under no circumstances should any hypothetical, placeholder, or synthesized mock data be used for clinic details, doctor names, patient reviews, rental rates, equipment pricing, or business metrics.

---

## 📋 Strict Empirical Standards

1. **Clinic Data & Doctor Names**
   - Must be extracted strictly from `bengaluru_physio_clinics.json` or verified Google Maps / Practo / JustDial listings.
   - Example: Dr. Pinky Dutta (PT), MPT RGUHS 2009, 17+ Yrs Exp for CorpErgo Physiotherapy.
   - Do NOT invent fake doctor names, fake qualifications, or fake address lines.

2. **Patient Reviews & Ratings**
   - Review counts, star ratings, and patient praise must be derived from actual verified Google Maps review text or scraped sentiment fields.
   - Quotations in mockups must reflect actual patient praise themes found in the clinic's public feedback.

3. **Rental Market Rates**
   - Rental quotes per sq ft and deposit norms must be sourced strictly from verified commercial listings (NoBroker, 99acres, MagicBricks, AddressAdvisors).
   - Ground floor vs internal layout pricing distinctions must be cited with actual market ranges.

4. **Equipment & Instrument Pricing**
   - Equipment price ranges (Basic, Medium, Advanced) must match verified manufacturer/distributor listings on IndiaMART and HospitalStore.

5. **Exact Google Maps Location Embed & Directions Link**
   - Every generated website MUST include an interactive map strip (`.map-strip`) with the clinic's exact latitude/longitude or place ID.
   - Must include an overlay card with the exact verified address and a direct "Get Directions" link pointing to the clinic's actual Google Maps URL or query.

6. **Mandatory Lead Website Verification Protocol**
   - BEFORE selecting any business or clinic lead to target or build a mockup for, you MUST perform a deep web & dataset check to verify that they do NOT already own a standalone website anywhere on the internet.
   - Run search queries: `"[Clinic Name]" + official website` or check WHOIS / GMB dataset fields.
   - If a clinic already owns a standalone `.com` / `.in` domain, SKIP them immediately and pick a verified 100% website-less target.

7. **Outreach & Pitches**
   - All pitch templates and live mockup demos must cite verified empirical data (exact address, real phone number, actual review count, real rating).

---

## 🔍 Pre-Generation Protocol

Before generating any clinic website, proposal, pitch script, or guide update:
1. **Query the local dataset & verify NO website exists** for the target lead.
2. **Run targeted web/WHOIS/GMB verification** if data is missing or incomplete.
3. **Inject only verified empirical facts** into the HTML, CSS, JS, or Markdown artifacts.
